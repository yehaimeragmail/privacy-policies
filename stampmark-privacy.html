Privacy Policy — StampMark

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This is the privacy policy for StampMark. It is written in plain words on purpose, so anyone can understand it at a glance.

About this app

StampMark is a photo watermark app for Apple devices (iOS). It adds watermarks to your photos, removes hidden location and camera details from photo files, and saves your watermark presets. It works fully offline. This policy applies to the Apple version of the app.

In short

  • StampMark collects no personal data at all.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, and no login of any kind.
  • There is no tracking, no third-party analytics, and no advertising in the Apple version.
  • Your photos are never uploaded, sent, or shared anywhere by this app. Everything happens on your device only.
  • Your photos, watermarks, and presets stay on your device only.

Your photos stay on your device

StampMark only opens the photos you choose yourself, on your own device, so it can add a watermark or clean them. Those photos never leave your device. They are not uploaded to a server, not sent to anyone, and not shared by this app in any way.

Some photos carry hidden details inside the file, such as the place where the photo was taken and the camera name and settings. StampMark reads those details only on your device, only so it can show them to you and remove them if you ask it to. Those details are never sent anywhere by this app.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, store, share, or sell any of the following, in the Apple version of StampMark:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, camera, or microphone
  • Any device identifier used for tracking you across apps or websites
  • Anything about how you browse the web or use other apps

Because none of this is collected, none of it can be shared with anyone else, and none of it can be sold. There is nothing to share and nothing to sell.

What stays on your device only

The only things the app keeps are the things it needs to work on your own device, for example your saved watermark presets and your settings. These are stored on your device only, using the device's own storage. They are not sent anywhere by this app. If you delete the app from your device, that on-device information is deleted with it.

Purchases

If StampMark offers an in-app purchase on Apple, that purchase is handled entirely by Apple. This app does not receive, see, or store your payment details, card details, or Apple account details. A purchase only unlocks features inside the app itself; it does not change anything in this policy, and it does not cause any personal data to be collected.

Children

For younger users: this app collects nothing from children, just as it collects nothing from adults. There is no sign-in, no chat, and no place to share personal details.

Changes to this policy

That covers everything. Should this policy ever change, the updated version will be posted at the same link first, and it will not reduce the protections described here without notice.
