Support — Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe is the classic game of X and O. Play against the computer, or play with a friend on the same device.

Contact us

If something is not working, or you have a question about the game, write to us at games@mrsaqlainproductions.com. Please tell us what happened, which screen you were on, and what device you use.

Common questions

The game will not start. Close the app fully and open it again. Make sure your device has enough free space.

There is no sound. Check that your device is not on silent, and that Sound effects are switched on in the game's Settings.

How do I play? You are X. Tap any empty square to place your X. The computer (O) answers. Get three of your marks in a row — across, down, or diagonal — to win. If the board fills with no winner, it is a draw.

Can I play with a friend? Yes. On the main menu tap Two Players. Two people take turns on the same device, X and O.

How do I change the table or the X and O pieces? Open Settings from the main menu or from inside a game. Pick a table theme and a marker style there.

How do I restore Tic Tac Toe PRO? Open the Tic Tac Toe PRO screen in the game and use Restore Purchases. PRO is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID. It unlocks all 10 table themes, all 6 marker styles, and the Hard bot.
