Privacy Policy — Iron Stack

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This is the privacy policy for Iron Stack. It is written in plain words on purpose, so anyone can understand it at a glance.

About this app

Iron Stack is a barbell plate calculator and powerlifting tracker for Apple devices (iOS). It works out which plates to load on a barbell, logs workout sets, and shows strength progress over time. It works fully offline. This policy applies to the Apple version of the app.

In short

  • Iron Stack collects no personal data at all.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, and no login of any kind.
  • There is no tracking, no third-party analytics, and no advertising in the Apple version.
  • Your workout data is never uploaded, sent, or shared anywhere by this app. Everything happens on your device only.
  • Your barbell and plate settings, your logged sets, and your progress stay on your device only.

Your workouts stay on your device

Iron Stack only uses the workout details you type in yourself, on your own device, so it can work out plate loads and show your progress. Those details are your barbell weight, your chosen plates and units, your logged sets (lift, weight and reps), your bodyweight, and your chosen training program. They never leave your device. They are not uploaded to a server, not sent to anyone, and not shared by this app in any way.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, store, share, or sell any of the following, in the Apple version of Iron Stack:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, camera, or microphone
  • Any device identifier used for tracking you across apps or websites
  • Anything about how you browse the web or use other apps

Because none of this is collected, none of it can be shared with anyone else, and none of it can be sold. There is nothing to share and nothing to sell.

What stays on your device only

The only things the app keeps are the things it needs to work on your own device, for example your barbell and plate settings, your logged sets, your bodyweight and progress, your active training program, and your settings. These are stored on your device only, using the device's own storage. They are not sent anywhere by this app. If you delete the app from your device, that on-device information is deleted with it.

Purchases

If Iron Stack offers an in-app purchase on Apple, that purchase is handled entirely by Apple. This app does not receive, see, or store your payment details, card details, or Apple account details. A purchase only unlocks features inside the app itself; it does not change anything in this policy, and it does not cause any personal data to be collected.

Children

For younger users: this app collects nothing from children, just as it collects nothing from adults. There is no sign-in, no chat, and no place to share personal details.

Changes to this policy

That covers everything. Should this policy ever change, the updated version will be posted at the same link first, and it will not reduce the protections described here without notice.
