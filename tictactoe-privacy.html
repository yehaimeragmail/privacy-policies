Privacy Policy — Tic Tac Toe

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Welcome to the privacy policy for Tic Tac Toe. Here is the whole story in one line: this game collects no personal information at all.

About this game

Tic Tac Toe is a classic board game for Apple devices (iOS). You play it on your own device, and it works offline. This policy applies to the Apple version of the game.

In short

  • Tic Tac Toe collects no personal data at all.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, and no login of any kind.
  • There is no tracking, no third-party analytics, and no advertising in the Apple version.
  • Nothing you do in the game is sent to a server by this game.
  • Your game progress, scores, and settings stay on your device only.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, store, share, or sell any of the following, in the Apple version of Tic Tac Toe:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Any device identifier used for tracking you across apps or websites
  • Anything about how you browse the web or use other apps

Because none of this is collected, none of it can be shared with anyone else, and none of it can be sold. There is nothing to share and nothing to sell.

What stays on your device only

The only things the game keeps are the things it needs to play on your own device, for example your game progress, your best scores, and your sound and display settings. These are stored on your device only, using the device's own storage. They are not sent anywhere by this game. If you delete the game from your device, that on-device information is deleted with it.

Purchases

If Tic Tac Toe offers an in-app purchase on Apple, that purchase is handled entirely by Apple. This game does not receive, see, or store your payment details, card details, or Apple account details. A purchase only unlocks content inside the game itself; it does not change anything in this policy, and it does not cause any personal data to be collected.

Children

A note for parents and guardians: because nothing is collected, there is nothing here that could identify your child. You can also use the device's own Screen Time and purchase controls for extra peace of mind.

Changes to this policy

And that is all there is to it. Any future change to this policy will be published at the same repository link before it takes effect.
