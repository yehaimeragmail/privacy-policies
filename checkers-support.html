Support — Checkers

Checkers is the classic board game of jumps and kings. Play against the computer, or play with a friend on the same device.

Contact us

If something is not working, or you have a question about the game, write to us at games@mrsaqlainproductions.com. Please tell us what happened, which screen you were on, and what device you use.

Common questions

The game will not start. Close the app fully and open it again. Make sure your device has enough free space.

There is no sound. Check that your device is not on silent, and that Sound effects are switched on in the game's Settings.

How do I play? Tap one of your discs, then tap a square to move it. Jump over an opponent's disc to capture it. Reach the far side of the board to make a king. Capture all of your opponent's discs, or block them so they cannot move, to win.

Can I play with a friend? Yes. On the main menu tap Play, then 2 Players. Two people take turns on the same device.

How do I change the board or the discs? Open Settings from the main menu or from inside a game. Pick a tavern theme and a disc style there.

How do I restore Checkers PRO? Open the Checkers PRO screen in the game and use Restore Purchases. PRO is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID. It unlocks all 9 tavern themes, all 5 disc styles, and the Hard bot.
