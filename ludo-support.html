Support — Ludo

Ludo is a classic board game for 2–4 players. Play against friends on the same device, or against the computer.

Contact us

If something is not working, or you have a question about the game, write to us at games@mrsaqlainproductions.com. Please tell us what happened, which screen you were on, and what device you use.

Common questions

The game will not start. Close the app fully and open it again. Make sure your device has enough free space.

There is no sound. Check that your device is not on silent, and that Music and Sound effects are switched on in the game's Settings.

How do I restore Ludo PRO? Open the Ludo PRO screen in the game and use Restore Purchases. Ludo PRO is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID.

How do I play? Tap the dice to roll, then tap one of your tokens to move it. Get all four of your tokens home first to win. Rolling a 6 gives you another turn.
