Privacy Policy — ZabanGo

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This is the privacy policy for ZabanGo. It is written in plain words on purpose, so anyone can understand it at a glance.

About this app

ZabanGo is an Urdu and English learning app for Apple devices (iOS). It teaches Urdu and English with short lessons, a phrasebook, grammar notes, stories and quizzes, and it reads phrases aloud so learners can hear real spoken Urdu. It works fully offline. This policy applies to the Apple version of the app.

In short

  • ZabanGo collects no personal data at all.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, and no login of any kind.
  • There is no tracking, no third-party analytics, and no advertising in the Apple version.
  • Nothing you learn, practise, or save is ever uploaded, sent, or shared anywhere by this app. Everything happens on your device only.
  • Your learning progress, hearts, gems, saved phrases, and settings stay on your device only.

Your learning stays on your device

ZabanGo only keeps the things it needs to remember your learning on your own device: which lessons you have finished, your hearts, gems, XP (points), day streak, saved phrases, your chosen voice persona, your learner name and picture, and your sound and script settings. These are stored on your device only, using the device's own storage. They are not sent anywhere by this app. If you delete the app from your device, that on-device information is deleted with it.

Spoken pronunciation stays on your device

ZabanGo reads phrases aloud using the phone's own built-in speech, on the device only. When you repeat a phrase out loud for the Speak & Repeat Coach, the phone's own speech recognition checks your pronunciation on the device only. The app does not record your voice, does not store your voice, and does not send your voice anywhere.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, store, share, or sell any of the following, in the Apple version of ZabanGo:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details
  • Your location
  • Your contacts or camera
  • Any recording of your voice
  • Any device identifier used for tracking you across apps or websites
  • Anything about how you browse the web or use other apps

Because none of this is collected, none of it can be shared with anyone else, and none of it can be sold. There is nothing to share and nothing to sell.

What stays on your device only

The only things the app keeps are the things it needs to work on your own device, for example your learning progress, hearts, gems, saved phrases, and your settings. These are stored on your device only, using the device's own storage. They are not sent anywhere by this app. If you delete the app from your device, that on-device information is deleted with it.

Purchases

If ZabanGo offers an in-app purchase on Apple, that purchase is handled entirely by Apple. This app does not receive, see, or store your payment details, card details, or Apple account details. A purchase only unlocks features inside the app itself; it does not change anything in this policy, and it does not cause any personal data to be collected.

Children

For younger users: this app collects nothing from children, just as it collects nothing from adults. There is no sign-in, no chat, and no place to share personal details.

Changes to this policy

That covers everything. Should this policy ever change, the updated version will be posted at the same link first, and it will not reduce the protections described here without notice.
